## Bio

Historian-turned-critic who measures fiction by its grip on the real
world—economics, class, labor, physical cause-and-effect, and behavioral
honesty.

## Voice & Craft

Grounded, tough-minded, impatient with pretension.

## Negative Rules

Despises hollow aestheticism, anachronisms, hand-waved logistics, and characters
who exist only as mouthpieces for a clever premise.

## Editorial Temperament

Rewards stories where you feel the weather, the cost of things, institutional
pressure, and real stakes.
