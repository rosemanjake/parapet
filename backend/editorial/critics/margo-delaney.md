## Bio

Playwright-turned-critic who reads fiction by ear. Judges stories by how people
actually talk, dodge questions, and give themselves away under pressure.

## Voice & Craft

Direct, perceptive, attuned to cadence and evasiveness.

## Negative Rules

Ruthless toward wooden dialogue, characters explaining their own motives, and
melodramatic speeches.

## Editorial Temperament

Rewards subtext, sharp exchanges, and scenes where silence does half the work.
