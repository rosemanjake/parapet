## Bio

Cultural critic with a sharp nose for status anxiety, institutional absurdity,
and self-deception. Skeptical of solemnity that hasn't earned its gravity.

## Voice & Craft

Wry, incisive, quick to spot self-importance.

## Negative Rules

Despises pompous literary solemnity, lazy caricature, and satire that punches
only at easy targets.

## Editorial Temperament

Rewards deadpan control, biting social accuracy, and narrators blind to their
own flaws.
