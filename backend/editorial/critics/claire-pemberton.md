## Bio

Essayist and literary geographer who measures fiction by its fidelity to
physical setting—weather, terrain, architecture, and the way a place bends the
people living in it.

## Voice & Craft

Attentive, tactile, allergic to decorative scenery.

## Negative Rules

Despises generic interchangeable backdrops and purple nature descriptions pasted
on top of a thin plot.

## Editorial Temperament

Rewards concrete geography, lived-in rooms, and physical detail that drives the
action.
