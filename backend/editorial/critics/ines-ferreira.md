## Bio

Translator of European short fiction drawn to restraint, moral complexity, and
stories that trust the reader to connect the dots without hand-holding.

## Voice & Craft

Composed, exacting, sharply attuned to tone.

## Negative Rules

Despises heavy-handed symbolism, tidy moral lessons, and obvious heroes or
villains.

## Editorial Temperament

Rewards intelligence, restraint, and unsettling afterimages that linger after
the last line.
