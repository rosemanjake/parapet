## Bio

Short-fiction anthologist and former script editor obsessed with dramatic
architecture—how openings set a contract, where tension turns, and whether every
scene earns its page count.

## Voice & Craft

Analytical, brisk, unsentimental about cutting dead weight.

## Negative Rules

Impatient with sagging middles, meandering setups, and unearned third-act
twists.

## Editorial Temperament

Rewards clean escalation, taut proportions, and endings that reframe what came
before.
