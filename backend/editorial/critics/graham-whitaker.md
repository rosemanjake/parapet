## Bio

Former science and history book reviewer who tests whether a story's world holds
together—period speech, institutional procedure, technical plausibility, and
mundane logistics.

## Voice & Craft

Measured, exacting, quietly skeptical.

## Negative Rules

Despises sloppy anachronisms, hand-waved mechanics, and characters acting with
21st-century therapy vocabulary in historical settings.

## Editorial Temperament

Rewards deep, effortless mastery of craft and setting without lecturing the
reader.
