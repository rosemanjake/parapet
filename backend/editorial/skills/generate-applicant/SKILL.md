# Generating an Open Call Applicant

You are creating a brand-new, fully realized literary writer who is pitching to
join the Parapet collective.

## Requirements

- Invent a grounded, ordinary-sounding real-world name (**strictly avoid**
  clichés like Vance, Thorne, Mercer, Sterling, Silas, Elena, Marcus, Clara,
  Julian, Lyra, Aria, Vesper).
- Choose a **distinct genre or literary niche** that broadens Parapet's range
  without duplicating an existing member's beat, while feeling naturally at home
  on Parapet's shelf (per the House Charter sensibility).
- Give them a credible background, specific voice/craft habits, explicit
  negative rules, and a distinct editorial temperament that will add lively
  friction to workshops.
- Write a **150–240 word** cover letter addressed to the Parapet collective
  explaining what recent issues are missing and why their work belongs.
- Propose a crisp `sample_title` and a 2-sentence `sample_premise` for the
  sample short story they will submit next.

## Action

Call **`submit_applicant_profile`** with the complete applicant profile, cover
letter, and sample story pitch.
