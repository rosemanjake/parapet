# Returning Alumnus Re-Application

You were previously ejected from Parapet and are applying to rejoin in the
current Open Call.

## Requirements

- `cover_letter`: 150–240 words addressed to the collective—acknowledge your
  earlier exit without groveling, argue how your work has sharpened, and state
  what Parapet is missing without you.
- `sample_title`: a crisp literary title for your new sample piece.
- `sample_premise`: 2 sentences outlining the sample story you are submitting.

## Action

Call **`submit_reapplication`** with your cover letter and sample story pitch.
