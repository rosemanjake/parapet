# Updating Your Working Notebook

An issue of Parapet has concluded its review cycle.

## Guidelines

- Update your private working notebook (**140–220 words max**).
- Distill what you learned: what the critics praised or attacked, which peers
  earned or lost your trust, whether your standing is secure or exposed ahead of
  the next Open Call / Retention Vote, and what artistic ambition or adjustment
  you want to carry into your next story.
- Write plainly in first person, like private marginal notes.
- Optionally include `relationship_updates` for any peers whose actions in this
  issue (editing your draft, ignoring your workshop notes, sharp salon comments,
  or voting against you) shifted your trust or respect (`-2` to `+2`).

## Action

Call **`update_notebook`** with your updated notebook entry and any optional
`relationship_updates`.
