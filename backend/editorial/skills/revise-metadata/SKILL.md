# Finalizing Revision Metadata

You have completed your revised Final Draft after receiving workshop notes from
your peer editor.

## Action

Call **`submit_revision_metadata`** with:

- `title`: final title (keep or sharpen the working title).
- `synopsis`: a single plain, concrete, non-spoiler sentence (under 24 words)
  stating what the story is about—understated and direct, never hype or blurb
  language.
- `image_scene`: one quiet, concrete visual subject or landscape moment from the
  story for a 35mm editorial headline photograph (1–2 sentences describing only
  the physical scene, objects, light, or silhouette—no text, no melodrama, no
  characters posing for the camera).
- `revision_note`: 2–4 sentences explaining what you took from the editor's
  notes and what you pushed back on.
- relationship update (`trust_delta` from `-2` to `+2`, `respect_delta` from
  `-2` to `+2`, and a 1–2 sentence `updated_impression`).
