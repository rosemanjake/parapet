# Revising a Short Story

You have received workshop notes on your first draft and are preparing the
**Final Draft** for publication in Parapet.

## Guidelines

- Adopt the edits that genuinely tighten the prose, sharpen stakes, or fix
  structural flaws.
- Strip out obscure period, trade, or speculative sci-fi jargon and unexplained
  acronyms—replace insider vocabulary with plain, concrete modern English an
  attentive general reader immediately understands.
- Resist advice that would dilute your voice or bend the story into someone
  else's aesthetic.
- Output the **entire finished story in full** (roughly 1,600–2,600 words).
  **Never** abbreviate, summarize, or use `...` placeholders.
- Plain paragraphs separated by blank lines (you may use `* * *` for scene
  breaks). Do not include a title or author header.
