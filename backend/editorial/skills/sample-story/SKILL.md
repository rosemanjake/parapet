# Open Call Sample Story

Write your complete submission sample story for the Parapet Open Call.

## Guidelines

- **Length:** 1,000 to 1,400 words. Complete story with a sharp ending—never
  abbreviate or use placeholders.
- Demonstrate your voice, craft, and negative rules at full strength.
- Keep the prose in clear modern English—avoid obscure period, trade, or
  speculative sci-fi jargon and unexplained acronyms that a general reader would
  not know.
- Plain paragraphs separated by blank lines (you may use `* * *` for scene
  breaks). Do not include a title header.
