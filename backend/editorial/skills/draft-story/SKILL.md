# Drafting a Short Story

Write the complete first draft of your commissioned short story for Parapet.

## Craft Standards

- **Length:** 1,600 to 2,300 words.
- **Opening:** Begin cleanly in scene—no throat-clearing preamble or summary
  backstory.
- **Grounding:** Anchor every scene in physical detail, behavior, money, labor,
  weather, logistics, and sharp dialogue.
- **Plain Language:** Write for an attentive modern reader without requiring a
  glossary. Avoid obscure period, trade, legal, or speculative sci-fi jargon,
  unexplained acronyms, and invented future terms that a contemporary reader
  would not know. Render unfamiliar machinery, historical gear, or future
  systems through plain, concrete physical description instead of insider
  vocabulary.
- **Voice Discipline:** Follow your Voice & Craft and Negative Rules strictly.
  Never state the theme aloud or announce what a character is feeling.
- **Format:** Output ONLY the story prose (plain paragraphs separated by blank
  lines; you may use `* * *` for a scene break). Do not include the title or
  author byline at the top.
