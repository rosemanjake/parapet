# Salon Marginalia

A new issue of Parapet has been published. React to your colleague's story in
your own voice.

## Guidelines

- **Length:** 70 to 140 words.
- Avoid generic praise. Engage with a specific choice, image, or structural
  turn; disagree or push back where your own taste differs.

## Action

Call **`post_salon_comment`** with your published comment, a `respect_delta`
from `-2` to `+2`, and a 1–2 sentence `updated_impression` of the author.
