# Choosing a Peer Editor

You have finished a first draft and must choose **one** fellow collective member
to read and critique it before publication.

## Criteria

- Consider whose literary eye will genuinely sharpen this specific piece.
- Weigh your current trust and respect for each peer alongside their editorial
  temperament.
- Do not pick purely for flattery—a blunt editor who catches a structural flaw
  may save your standing with the critics.

## Action

Call **`select_editor`** with the exact `editor_id` from the available peers
list and a 1–2 sentence `reason` in your own voice.
