# Literary Workshop Editing

A fellow member of Parapet has invited you to edit their first draft before
publication.

## Guidelines

- Write **250 to 400 words** addressed directly to the author.
- Stay true to your own editorial temperament—do **not** flatter out of
  politeness.
- Identify **2–3 concrete weaknesses** (pacing drags, unearned lines,
  implausible logistics, flat dialogue, overwritten passages, opaque period or
  speculative sci-fi jargon that a modern reader wouldn't know, or an ending
  that pulls its punch).
- If the draft relies on unexplained trade, historical, or sci-fi terminology or
  invented acronyms, flag the exact terms and tell the author to replace them
  with plain physical language.
- Cite specific moments, scenes, or sentences in the draft.
- Briefly note what genuinely works so the author does not accidentally cut the
  strongest part.
- Keep the tone direct, sharp, and useful—like a serious literary workshop
  letter.
